Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Fast Collect Challenge ("we", "our", or "us") operates the website and application hosted at fastcollectchallenge.com. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our game and authentication services (including Google OAuth and Firebase).

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with access to the game, save your progression, and secure your account using OAuth services (such as Google or GitHub), we collect the following information:

  • Account Credentials: Username, email address, and unique account ID provided through Google, GitHub, or Firebase authentication.
  • Game Progress & Inventory: Collected items, score history, transaction records, and in-game achievements stored via Google Firestore.
  • Technical Data: Standard connection metadata required for real-time services and application performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • To create, manage, and secure your player account.
  • To synchronize your game progression and inventories securely via cloud databases (Firestore).
  • To enable community features such as item trading between players.
  • To maintain and improve game performance and provide technical support.

3. Data Sharing and Security

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personally identifiable information to third parties. All data is securely hosted on Google Firebase infrastructure. We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

4. Third-Party Services

Our application uses Google OAuth for authentication purposes. By signing in with Google, you authorize us to access basic profile information (such as your email and name) in accordance with Google's API Services User Data Policy.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, you can contact the site administrator at felixrougee@gmail.com or via our official community channels.
